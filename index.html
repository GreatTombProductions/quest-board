The Impossible Percentages Audit — hyperlocal demographic shares that break arithmetic

The No-Upstream-Fix Triage — Severity Alarm Without Fix-Availability or Reachability

The Baseline-Fingerprint Lock — Making 'Did the Number Move, or the Math?' a Machine Check (Silent Revision Watch mechanism)

Analytics vendors and index publishers profit from opacity — a silent re-weight or universe-filter change tunes published numbers toward business goals while reading as market movement to readers; the vigilance market itself fails because watchdogs cry wolf and die of alert fatigue. Renters negotiating renewals, homebuyers making offers, and small landlords pricing units benefit from a machine-checkable, attention-free-until-change signal that says whether a moved number means the world moved or the math moved — without trusting vendor disclosure.

Noticed during the s53 supply-chain audit (kyouhukou, 2026-08-19): a SHA-256 fingerprint over the HIGH/CRITICAL finding identity set (path|package|severity) came back byte-identical across three independent sessions — silence is health, no re-routing; a change would have arrived as a diff localizing exactly which entries moved. Project shape: a static observatory that pins such a fingerprint over each tracked series' (publisher, series, methodology-version, universe-definition) identity tuple and re-computes it on every refresh. Fingerprint unchanged = the number may move but the math didn't — no alarm, zero maintenance attention (the property that keeps public watchdogs from dying of alert fatigue). Fingerprint changed = the diff IS the analysis: new data on unchanged math (real signal) vs silent methodology revision (math moved). Output: one line per series — LOCKED / DATA-MOVED / MATH-MOVED with the diff.

The Count-Referent Binding Audit — When the Number Is Real but Counts the Wrong Thing

Platforms and agencies profit when record-level counts read as referent-level facts: '3,000 providers in network' where record duplicates inflate the roster, review counts where multiple entries from one experience read as independent signals, 'N active listings' where variant strings split or merge at will. The consumer at the decision boundary (patient choosing in-network care, buyer comparing options, renter estimating availability) over-trusts or under-trusts the market because the count's binding is invisible. Transparency benefit: the count keeps its real value while the binding becomes visible — 'these 3,000 records are 2,412 distinct providers, of which 1,911 match your plan.'

A static auditor for any public count metric (platform 'N providers in network,' 'N reviews,' 'N verified listings,' review-count badges, review-aggregate denominators): for each displayed count, recompute it against an independently ground-truthed referent set and render the three-way divergence — records vs referents vs what the consumer at the decision boundary needs. The output names the binding, not just the number: which records collapsed into one referent (shared-meal counting), which referent split across record variants (hyphen/case/duplicate rows), and which denominator the count silently includes that the consumer's question excludes (entity profiles + spawn classes in an 'agent' count). Pattern-shaped, not site-specific: any finder, marketplace, or status surface where a specific count reads as capacity.
